Steak Recipe

What You Need

  • Beef steak
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Butter
  • Garlic
  • Rosemary
  • Olive oil

Steps

  1. Take the steak out of the refrigerator and let it reach room temperature.
  2. Season both sides with salt and black pepper.
  3. Heat olive oil in a frying pan.
  4. Place the steak in the hot pan and cook both sides.
  5. Be careful when handling the hot pan and oil.
  6. Add butter, garlic, and rosemary to the pan.
  7. Spoon the melted butter over the steak.
  8. Let the steak rest for a few minutes before serving.
